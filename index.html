About

Between the clinic and the codebase

A computer scientist by training, retraining now in health data.

I studied Computer Science at Korea University and spent the years after graduating in the parts of the industry where software meets scale — a live mobile game with players in over 230 countries, an NLP company building question-answering APIs, and an academy whose book catalogue had outgrown its spreadsheets. The common thread was never the domain. It was that somebody needed a system that made a pile of data legible.

Healthcare is where that problem gets serious. Clinical data is fragmented, inconsistently coded, and the cost of getting it wrong is not a bad quarter. That is why I moved to Singapore for the MSc in Biomedical Informatics at NUS — to pair the engineering I already have with the clinical and statistical grounding I did not.

I am most useful somewhere between the two: close enough to the data to know what it will not tell you, close enough to production to ship the thing anyway.
